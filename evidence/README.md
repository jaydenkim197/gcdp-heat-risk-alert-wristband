# Development Evidence

This directory preserves the project evidence that complements the dated
engineering logs in [`../docs/`](../docs/).

## Contents

- `hardware-experiments/`: bench setup photos, early sensor/LTE experiments,
  and enclosure-reference images.
- `3d-printing/`: print-quality evidence, printer and filament settings, and
  the leveling-test STL used during prototype fabrication.
- `demo/`: final outdoor prototype demonstration video.
- `process-screenshots/`: chronological screenshots from the CAD, slicing, and
  prototype assembly process, plus date-level contact sheets.
- `3d-scan/`: final prototype 3D scan for geometry review.

## Related Engineering Logs

- `../docs/260708_hardware_bringup_log.md`: MCU and I2C sensor bring-up.
- `../docs/260709_lte_sms_integrated_firmware_log.md`: LTE, GNSS, SMS, and
  power/UART recovery tests.
- `../docs/260713_battery_standalone_alert_test_log.md`: battery-powered
  standalone alert-flow test.
- `../docs/260731_stamp_catm_bringup_diagnostic.md`: Stamp CatM diagnostic
  measurements and UART validation.
- `../docs/260801_BE-220_bringup_and_fix_log.md`: external GNSS baud-rate
  diagnosis and outdoor-fix validation.

## Scope Note

The artifacts document a functional educational prototype, not a certified
medical device. Third-party datasheets, downloaded reference models, purchase
records, and personal identifiers are intentionally excluded from this public
repository.
